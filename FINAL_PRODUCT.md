# Cipher CV — Final Product

> **Privacy-first job matching powered by Fully Homomorphic Encryption.**  
> 8 smart contracts. 3 testnets. Zero plaintext.

---

## Product Summary

Cipher CV is a decentralized job matching protocol that computes compatibility between candidates and employers entirely on encrypted data. Neither party ever learns the other's salary range, experience level, or skill requirements — until both explicitly consent to reveal.

Built on Fhenix's fhEVM (the first EVM with native FHE), Cipher CV is the first production application to use homomorphic encryption for real-world job matching.
